## 📽️ Scene-by-Scene Production Script

---

### **Scene 1: The Problem — The Notification Avalanche**
* **Duration:** `00:00 – 00:07` (7s)
* **Pacing:** Fast, chaotic, tense
* **Mood:** Frustration with disconnected smart-camera alerts

#### 👁️ Visual & Animation:
- **Environment:** Pitch-black background (`#0A0B0D`) with subtle radial dark vignette.
- **Hero Prop:** 3D smartphone floating slightly tilted at an angle.
- **Animation Sequence:**
  1. The phone's lock screen buzzes repeatedly.
  2. Rapid Ring motion banners cascade onto the screen at high speed:
     - `10:14 AM — Motion at Front Gate`
     - `10:15 AM — Motion on Walkway`
     - `10:16 AM — Motion at Front Door`
     - `10:17 AM — Doorbell Ring`
  3. Banners stack, jitter, and blur into a chaotic, unreadable pile.
  4. Red notification badge counts surge: `+1`, `+5`, `+18`.
- **On-Screen Text (Kinetic Typography):**
  > `DOZENS OF CLIPS. ZERO CONTEXT.` (Strobes white, then cuts to dark).

#### 🔊 Audio & Sound FX:
- **SFX:** Rapid overlapping mobile alerts (*ding-ding-ping-ping*), subtle electrical hum.
- **Voiceover (VO):**
  > *"Your smart cameras record everything... but tell you nothing."*

---

### **Scene 2: The Solution — Reveal of GateStory**
* **Duration:** `00:07 – 00:15` (8s)
* **Pacing:** Sudden transition to clean, smooth calm
* **Mood:** Relief, sophistication, clarity

#### 👁️ Visual & Animation:
- **Camera Move:** Rapid rack-focus zoom. The chaotic notifications shatter into glowing micro-particles and dissolve.
- **Animation Sequence:**
  1. An electric wash of **Coinbase/GateStory Blue (`#0052FF`)** sweeps across the screen.
  2. The phone unlocks with a crisp circular biometric ripple.
  3. The phone glides upright into centered isometric 3D view.
  4. The GateStory mobile interface loads:
     - Top pill badge pulses: `● Ring API: Live`
     - Clean, calm headline fades in:
       *Turn scattered clips into cohesive visit stories.*
- **Lighting:** Soft studio edge-lighting outlines the curved bevel of the phone chassis.

#### 🔊 Audio & Sound FX:
- **Music:** A deep sub-bass drop clears out the chaotic soundscape; a warm ambient electronic groove kicks in.
- **SFX:** Glass particle dissolve whoosh, smooth biometric unlock chime.
- **Voiceover (VO):**
  > *"Meet GateStory. The multi-camera visit correlation engine."*

---

### **Scene 3: The Magic — Spatial Path & Live Storyline**
* **Duration:** `00:15 – 00:26` (11s)
* **Pacing:** Engaging, tactile, explanatory
* **Mood:** High-tech, effortless understanding

#### 👁️ Visual & Animation:
- **Camera Move:** Macro camera glide over the mobile display.
- **Animation Sequence:**
  1. A simulated visit event triggers on the phone screen.
  2. A frosted glass card slides in with soft spring physics:
     - **Spatial Node Chain:** 3 circles connect with an animated laser pulse:
       `[0: Front Gate]` ➔ `[1: Walkway]` ➔ `[2: Front Door]`
  3. Tiny floating snapshot HUD thumbnails pop up above each hop, framing the visitor's path.
  4. Beneath the chain, the synthesized narrative types out dynamically:
     > *"Visitor arrived at Front Gate, proceeded along the walkway (21s), reached the front door (15s dwell), rang the doorbell, and departed. Total visit duration: 23s."*
  5. Green status badge stamps into place: `[reached_door_completed]`.

#### 🔊 Audio & Sound FX:
- **SFX:** Soft tactile blips as nodes illuminate (*blip... blip... ping!*), faint mechanical typewriter tick, crisp completion chime.
- **Voiceover (VO):**
  > *"Instead of isolated motion pings, GateStory correlates the exact spatial trajectory from gate to door in real time."*

---

### **Scene 4: Intelligence — Dwell Heatmaps & MCP AI Agent**
* **Duration:** `00:26 – 00:36` (10s)
* **Pacing:** Dynamic, feature-dense
* **Mood:** Powerful, assistant-ready

#### 👁️ Visual & Animation:
- **Camera Move:** Perspective tilt; UI panels elevate slightly above the screen in 3D parallax layers (Z-axis separation).
- **Split Motion Feature:**
  1. **Spatial Dwell Meter:** The heatmap drawer expands on mobile.
     - Progress bars smoothly grow to reflect average dwell seconds:
       - `Front Gate: 4s` (Cyan)
       - `Walkway: 8s` (Blue)
       - `Front Door: 15s` (Amber highlight)
  2. **AI Voice / MCP Assistant:**
     - An animated audio waveform bubble appears at the bottom.
     - Prompt pops up: *"Did anyone leave a package while I was away?"*
     - The `get_visit_story` MCP badge flashes with `<500ms` response indicator.
     - The AI assistant speaks the answer directly into the chat bubble.

#### 🔊 Audio & Sound FX:
- **SFX:** Tactile drawer slide, fluid meter filling swoosh, AI assistant activation ding.
- **Voiceover (VO):**
  > *"Identify dwell time patterns, and query your home's visit history through Claude, GPT, or any AI assistant with native MCP tools."*

---

### **Scene 5: Trust, Privacy by Design & Outro**
* **Duration:** `00:36 – 00:45` (9s)
* **Pacing:** Confident, grounded, memorable
* **Mood:** Complete peace of mind

#### 👁️ Visual & Animation:
- **Camera Move:** Camera pulls back smoothly to reveal the full floating phone.
- **Animation Sequence:**
  1. The screen transitions into a dark privacy shield lockup.
  2. A glowing blue outline of a security lock appears with 3 animated checkmarks:
     - `✓ No Facial Recognition`
     - `✓ No Biometric Watchlists`
     - `✓ Pure Spatial-Temporal Privacy`
  3. The phone settles gently onto an obsidian reflective surface.
  4. Final title card animates in above the device:
     ```
     GateStory
     Multi-Camera Smart Home Visit Storylines
     ```
  5. Secondary badge: `Amazon Hackathon 2026 · Track 4: Ring + AWS`
  6. Final URL tag: `localhost:4321 / gatestory.dev`

#### 🔊 Audio & Sound FX:
- **SFX:** Solid metallic lock click (`thud-snap`), crystalline audio tail.
- **Music:** Resolves into a warm, inspiring sustained synth pad.
- **Voiceover (VO):**
  > *"Track visits. Not identities. GateStory — Your home, finally telling the whole story."*

## 🛠️ Motion Designer & 3D Animator Production Specs

| Parameter | Recommended Specification |
|---|---|
| **Software Stack** | After Effects + Cinema 4D / Blender / Spline 3D |
| **Phone Model** | iPhone 16 Pro (Space Black) or Pixel 9 Pro with matte titanium finish |
| **Primary Palette** | Canvas: `#0A0B0D` \| Electric Blue: `#0052FF` \| Green: `#098551` \| Amber: `#B45309` \| White: `#FFFFFF` |
| **Animation Easing** | UI transitions: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` (spring) <br> Camera paths: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (smooth deceleration) |
| **Typography** | Inter Display (Font weights: 400 for calm headings, 700 for data badges, 500 for narrative) |
| **Voiceover Actor** | Calm, articulate, modern tech tone (Apple/Stripe keynote style) |
